# Optional Repair & Reconstruction Notes

Load only when deciding whether a causal error warrants reconstruction. Not a mandatory preflight.

## Reconstruction

Require reconstruction when answer-bearing teaching repaired a causal or foundational model. Good reconstruction asks the learner to rebuild the specific repaired relation in their own words — not a yes/no acknowledgment.

Do not require reconstruction for ordinary slips, minor terminology polish, or harmless rephrasing.

## Stop rules

Stop when the responsible failure boundary is localized, the minimal repair resolves the faulty model, the learner reconstructs or explicitly opts out, or interview/mock coaching must wait for debrief.

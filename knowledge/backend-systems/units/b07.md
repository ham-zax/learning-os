## Interactive case
A client writes successfully and reads a lagging replica; failover happens during a retry. Let the learner specify the product's actual read guarantee and choose among trade-offs. Use an unfamiliar domain rather than replaying the previous booking story word for word.

The teacher answers the learner's questions about the case, asks for the decisive prediction or decision and waits. The learner may disagree, request a reference, choose another practical context or reverse roles and review the teacher's proposed approach. A requested observation must have a consistent answer; do not change hidden facts to manufacture failure.

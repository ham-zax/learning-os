# Calibrated prediction and scaffold variant audit (A1)

**Date / scope:** 2026-10-11; non-ARM WSL checkout `/home/hamza/repo/learning-os`, starting at `3377309`. Reviewed all six published `knowledge/{frontend-revision,backend-systems}/challenges/*/calibration.json` packs (exactly 18 cases: six `same`, six `variant`, six `transfer`) and all four existing attached `scaffold/scaffold.json` packs. No real learner database or profile was opened for this content audit.

**Evidence method.** I read each `calibration.json`, its corresponding trusted `.mjs` source and question/rubric, plus `src/knowledge/challenge-calibration.ts`, `src/knowledge/scaffolds.ts` and the calibration/scaffold tests. Every assessment source was executed with `node <absolute-source-path>` via a bounded Node child process (5 s limit); the captured stdout lines were compared in order against `expectedOutput`. Each declared `wrongModels` prediction was compared to those real lines; partial claims were checked at the correct position (`predictedFinal` against the final displayed value, `predictedFirstUpdate` against line one). All 18 outputs matched and all **30 wrong-model contrasts** differ from their observed target. The eight scaffold `.mjs` check files were also executed with Node and their manifest outputs matched. `npm test` independently exercises execution, source/manifest separation, display/exposure and restart semantics.

Notation in the tables: `→` separates actual printed lines, **A** = actual stdout, **W** = the specified incorrect model's predicted stdout (or specified partial prediction). Brackets are literal printed arrays; values are not a claim about PostgreSQL, real queues or human performance. Each case's source is relative to its pack directory. `keep` means correct for this bounded *engineering* audit, not validated teaching gains.

**Variant verdict:** Each of the six designated `variant` cases alters a decision-bearing condition, not just names/numbers/story. The six `transfer` cases apply the mechanism to a different surface/invariant or ownership boundary. Printed output divergence alone cannot establish learner discrimination, but the respective case's false rule makes a distinct observable prediction. The structured `calibration.json` oracles are still teacher-only and are not interpolated into the public challenge question.

## Engineering disposition

**No observed calibration/content defect requiring a code change.** All 18 published cases are executable and discriminating under their stated Node models; all eight scaffold executable oracles and published learner/teacher boundaries pass. The alternate original-plan concern about superficial variation was checked rather than assumed. Accordingly no speculative loader API, schema, test harness or framework was added. Human comprehension, long-term retention, production DB semantics and real user-specific teaching outcomes were **not** tested here.
